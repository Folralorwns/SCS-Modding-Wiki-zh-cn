# Contribution

Hi there! As you have entered this page you are probably interested in helping us create articles for our modding wiki project.

To become a *contributor* to the **SCS Modding Wiki** all you need to do is write a short mail to us and we will try to reply to you as soon as possible.

## Who do we need?

We need people who are willing to help with creating articles for this wiki.

Content we are looking for at the moment:

- Any kind of tutorial related to SCS games.
- Any kind of written user guides for SCS games.
- Any possible improvements to existing documentation.

## What do you need to do?

All you need to do is write a short e-mail to `contributionscssoftcom` about who you are and what you would you like to contribute to.

We will then reply to you with further instructions.
