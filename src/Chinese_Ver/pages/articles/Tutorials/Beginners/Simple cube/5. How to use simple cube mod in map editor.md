| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | [![Simple_cube_ensure_console.jpg](../../../../../../images/articles/Tutorials/Beginners/Simple%20cube/5.%20How%20to%20use%20simple%20cube%20mod%20in%20map%20editor/Simple_cube_ensure_console.jpg)](../../../../files/Simple%20cube%20ensure%20console.jpg.md) | 打开游戏主目录中的 **config.cfg** 文件，并确保 **g_console** 和 **g_minicon** 均设置为 **"1"**。参见 [游戏用户路径](../../../Documentation/Engine/Game%20user%20path.md)，了解在你的计算机上何处可以找到 **config.cfg** 文件。 |
| **2** | [![Simple_cube_console.jpg](../../../../../../images/articles/Tutorials/Beginners/Simple%20cube/5.%20How%20to%20use%20simple%20cube%20mod%20in%20map%20editor/Simple_cube_console.jpg)](../../../../files/Simple%20cube%20console.jpg.md) | 启动游戏，然后按下键盘上的波浪号键 **[~]**。此时应出现游戏控制台。输入 **edit** 命令以启动地图编辑器。 |
| **3** | [![Simple_cube_select_item_type.jpg](../../../../../../images/articles/Tutorials/Beginners/Simple%20cube/5.%20How%20to%20use%20simple%20cube%20mod%20in%20map%20editor/Simple_cube_select_item_type.jpg)](../../../../files/Simple%20cube%20select%20item%20type.jpg.md) | 将 **item type** 更改为 **model**。 |
| **4** | [![Simple_cube_select_place_tool_and_select_model.jpg](../../../../../../images/articles/Tutorials/Beginners/Simple%20cube/5.%20How%20to%20use%20simple%20cube%20mod%20in%20map%20editor/Simple_cube_select_place_tool_and_select_model.jpg)](../../../../files/Simple%20cube%20select%20place%20tool%20and%20select%20model.jpg.md) | 点击绿色的 **+** 图标（**1**）选择 **item creation** 模式。将出现 item creation 窗口，选择你要创建的模型（**2**），在本例中为 **simple_cube/simple_cube.pmd**。点击**鼠标左键**将模型放置到世界中，然后点击 item creation 对话框并按键盘上的 **Escape** 键将其关闭。使用键盘上的 **C** 键将相机切换为 **bird camera**，然后飞到你的方块处，在游戏中查看它。 |
| **5** | [![Simple_cube_box_in_world.jpg](../../../../../../images/articles/Tutorials/Beginners/Simple%20cube/5.%20How%20to%20use%20simple%20cube%20mod%20in%20map%20editor/Simple_cube_box_in_world.jpg)](../../../../files/Simple%20cube%20box%20in%20world.jpg.md) | 恭喜。现在你可以在游戏中的地图上放置自己的模型了。干得不错！ |
